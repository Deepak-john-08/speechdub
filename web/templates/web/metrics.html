pitch detection algorithm — speaker_profiler.py · estimate_pitch()
autocorrelation-based F0 estimation
parametervalue from codepurpose
frame_length int(sr × 0.025) = 400 samples 25ms analysis window — captures ~2-3 pitch periods per frame
hop_length int(sr × 0.010) = 160 samples 10ms step — 75% frame overlap for smooth pitch tracking
min_period int(sr / 400) = 40 samples upper pitch limit 400 Hz — above human voice range
max_period int(sr / 60) = 267 samples lower pitch limit 60 Hz — below typical human vocal range
energy threshold RMS < 0.01 → skip silence detection — prevents pitch errors on silent frames
peak threshold autocorr[peak] > 0.3 × autocorr[0] voiced detection — rejects unvoiced/noisy frames
output np.median(pitches) median pitch across all valid frames — robust to outliers
gender boundary <165 Hz=male · >180 Hz=female detect_gender_from_pitch() · 165-180 Hz overlap → defaults male
